Your BCA Program Guide
BCA

Your program, in your pocket

A quick tour of your VIP program page. Five minutes now and you'll know where everything is.

Step 1

Log in

Go to bca.p7athletes.com and type the password I sent you in the Athlete login box. Tap Open my program.

Leave Remember me on this device ticked and you won't need the password again on that phone.

Forgot your password? Message me and I'll resend it.
Step 2

Put it on your home screen

Do this once and your program opens like an app.

iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button (the square with the arrow), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome): tap the three dots at the top right, then Add to Home screen.

Step 3

Start on Today

Today shows everything you have to do today. Tap the circle to tick something off, or tap the row to open it.

Scroll down for This week, your weekly tracker. Every box you tick shows up here, so you can see your week at a glance.

Your ticks are saved on your phone. If you open the program on another phone or browser, it starts fresh there.
Step 4

Use the tabs at the bottom

Everything lives in the bar at the bottom of the screen. Tap through all of them the first time.

  • Today: your checklist and weekly tracker.
  • Morning: your morning routine, one step at a time. Use Next and Back at the bottom.
  • Night: your routine before bed.
  • Core: your core work and how to breathe through it.
  • Posing: your posing drills and the posing timer.
  • Notes: my cues for you and what we're working on.
Step 5

Follow your vacuum class

In your Morning routine, the sequence step has a timer that runs like a class. Set your inhale, exhale, vacuum hold and change posture times, then press Start class. It shows how long the whole class will take.

It takes you through every posture for you: 3 breaths, a short pause to stop, swallow and soften the belly, then the vacuum. You don't touch the phone until it's done. Pause and Stop are there if you need them.

Listen for these sounds:

Soft high toneBreathe in
Low toneBreathe out
Bright chimeVacuum
Double chimeRelease
Bell (3 notes)Change posture
Turn your ringer on and the volume up. The screen stays awake while a timer runs.
Step 6

Wind down on Night

The Night tab has your breathing timer for bed. Set your inhale and exhale, press Start, and follow the tones. A double chime tells you you're done.

You'll also find the Tuesday evening yoga class here. Join live in the community, or do the recording, at least once a week. Today will remind you until you tick it off.

Step 7

Practise posing on the clock

On the Posing tab, scroll to Pose rounds. Pick how long to hold your front pose and back pose, how long your transitions are, and how many rounds.

You walk in, hold your front pose until the chime, turn and hold your back pose until the chime, and repeat. The last 3 seconds of every turn tick down. After your last round, a double chime means walk out.

That's it

Open it every day, follow Today, and tick things off as you go. Your program may not have every timer shown here, since each one is built for you.
