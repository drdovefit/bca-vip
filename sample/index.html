2 · Morning: breathing

Choose one to do every day. The times are a guide. Don't think about the clock, think about the breath. When the timer goes off, you're done. Long exhales, starting from your pelvic floor and up. No gripping your top abs.

Block breathing

1 min
  • Face down, block on top of your front ribs. On your forearms, or your hands if you're comfortable.
  • Inhale into your back body.
  • Exhale and let it all go.

4 · Morning: Low Pressure Fitness

About 15 min. Hold 8 s, building to 10. LPF timer 3 in, 6 out.

Slow it down

Every vacuum
  • Slow down your vacuum. Think of it like a slow eccentric.
  • Take your full exhale, swallow, then open the ribs slowly to the sides and back.
  • Suck goes UP. Pull up, never in.
  • Try it without plugging your nose now.

5 · Posing

Posing

Any time.

The exhale is the pose

  • 2 breaths. The third exhale is the pose.
  • Make the sound "ha". Your exhale always starts from the pelvic floor and rises up: your pelvic floor lightly lifts like you're about to do a Kegel, then your lower TA comes in, then your middle TA, then your top TA. Always in that order. Then hold that.
  • Small inhales into your back or sides. Think of breathing into your armpits.
  • Long exhales. Go "shh" and lift the pelvic floor.

Front pose timer

Start at 10 s, build to at least 30 s
  • Stop when your top abs start to grip.
Posing routine timer

Walk in to walk off

Runs your routine the way the judges call it: walk in, front, back, front, back, front, thank you, walk off. Two tones = hold your pose. Three tones = move. Stop when your top abs start to grip.

6 · Core

Core

2 to 3 days a week, on your days off or lighter days. 2 rounds, about 20 to 30 min.

How to brace

Every move
  • Exhale from the pelvic floor up: lower TA, middle TA, top TA.
  • Then breathe under the brace.
  • Bracing in, never out.
  • If your top abs grip, start over.

DO

Every move
  • Inhale first. Ribs open to the back and sides.
  • Exhale on the hard part. Pelvic floor first, then the lower core comes in and up.
  • Exhale from the pelvic floor up: lower TA, middle TA, then top TA. Breathe under the brace.
  • Grow. Head on the ceiling.

DON'T

Every move
  • Don't hold your breath.
  • Don't grip your top abs. If they pop, make the move smaller.
  • Don't let your core dome. If it domes, make the move smaller.
  • Don't close your ribs and push down. That's squeezing the top of the juice box.
  • Don't brace out. Bracing in, never out.

8 · Show day

Show day

  • Relax, have fun and enjoy your time on stage. It goes by so fast.
  • Long exhales calm you down. Use them all day.
  • Morning: diaphragm release over your robe. Careful of the tan, hands in one spot, breathe into it.
  • Backstage: 3 to 5 lying vacuums with your feet up on something, then long exhales. You want to wake up your core, not tire it out.
  • No vacuums before you pose.
